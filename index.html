FAQ

Questions

Does Ledger hold or trade my funds?

No. Ledger only displays market data for assets you tell it you hold. It never takes custody, never trades on your behalf, and never processes deposits or withdrawals.

Where do the prices come from?

Live public market data, refreshed roughly every 30 seconds. Values move because real markets move — nothing here is simulated or guaranteed.

Which countries can use this?

Ledger is a data display tool, not a financial or custodial service, so it's built to be usable anywhere with no region restriction on sign-up.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — plans are month to month with no lock-in.

Ledger is a market-data display tool. Not financial advice. Not a broker, exchange, or custodian.
